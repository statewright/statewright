# Statewright Patent Pledge

**Effective Date:** May 13, 2026

Ben Cochran ("Pledgor") hereby makes the following commitment regarding
US Provisional Patent Application 64/054,240 and any nonprovisional
applications, continuations, divisionals, and patents issuing therefrom
that cover claims fully supported by the disclosure of that provisional
application ("Covered Patents").

## Pledge

Pledgor will not assert the Covered Patents against any of the following
("Covered Parties"):

  (a) Any individual or entity that independently implements the techniques
      described in the Covered Patents for non-commercial purposes;

  (b) Any individual or entity building or distributing software under an
      OSI-approved open source license or a source-available license that
      permits public inspection of source code, where such software is not
      offered as a commercial service competing with Statewright;

  (c) Academic researchers and non-commercial research institutions;

  (d) Any individual developer or single team within an organization
      running a self-hosted deployment of such techniques for internal
      use, not offered as a service to third parties. Multi-team,
      multi-tenant, or organization-wide deployments require a
      commercial license from the Pledgor;

regardless of whether such parties use Statewright software.

## Exclusions

This pledge does not extend to any person or entity that offers a
commercial hosted service, SaaS product, or distributed software
product that competes with or replicates the core functionality of
Statewright AI products.

## Defensive Termination

This pledge does not apply to any party that files, maintains, threatens,
or voluntarily participates in a claim for patent infringement against:
(i) Ben Cochran or Statewright AI, or (ii) any user or distributor of
Statewright technology. Upon dismissal of such claims, coverage under
this pledge may be restored at Pledgor's discretion. This termination
applies only to the party taking such action and does not affect
coverage for other Covered Parties.

## Transferability

Pledgor intends this pledge to be binding on any assignee, successor,
or transferee of the Covered Patents. Any transfer of the Covered Patents
shall be conditioned on the transferee agreeing in writing to be bound
by this pledge, or the pledge shall be recorded as an encumbrance on
the transferred patents.

## Irrevocability

Except as provided in the Defensive Termination section above, this
pledge is irrevocable.

## Disclaimer

This pledge is not a license to use Statewright software, which is
governed separately by applicable license terms. This pledge does not
imply any representation that the Covered Patents are valid, enforceable,
or that the listed activities do or do not infringe them.
